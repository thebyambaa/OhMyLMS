## Most useful patterns
1. **The enrolment is the atom; rosters are a filter.** Every platform keeps one student–course enrolment record. Groups, sections and cohorts are memberships layered on top (Moodle, Canvas, LearnDash, Absorb).
2. **Separate hierarchy from cohort.** An org tree (TalentLMS branch, Docebo branch, Absorb department, Khan district/school) is distinct from flat, many-to-many groups.
3. **A roster can be both an enrolment method and a filter.** Moodle cohort enrolment, Sensei cohorts and TalentLMS group mass-enrol all create individual enrolments. Moodle group keys and LifterLMS seat purchases place self-enrolling buyers straight into a group.
4. **Scoped visibility by role.** A leader or teacher sees only their groups (LearnDash, Moodle Separate groups, TalentLMS branches). There are also read-only analysts (Thinkific).
5. **One grid, row and column filters, saved presets.** Canvas filters rows (section, group) and columns (module, type, dates) and saves them as presets. TalentLMS rules (AND across, OR within) can be scheduled.
6. **Several views over the same data:** course × students (Moodle Grader, Canvas), single student or transcript (Moodle Single view, LearnDash Report Card, LifterLMS member report), and outcomes/mastery (Canvas LMG, Khan). Section-specific assignments and due dates show as greyed-out "not assigned" cells.

## Recommended model for OhMyLMS
**Tables**
- `enrollment(id, user_id, course_id, source[purchase|self|manual|roster|seat], source_ref, status[active|completed|expired|suspended], starts_at, ends_at)`. Exactly one row per user and course, however they got in.
- `roster(id, type[class|section|cohort|org_group], name, parent_org_id, term, owner_id)`. Many-to-many with users through `roster_member(roster_id, user_id, role[student|teacher|leader|analyst])`, and many-to-many with courses through `roster_course`. Adding a roster to a course creates or links enrolments with `source=roster`.
- `org_unit(id, parent_id, type[district|school|company|dept])`. A tree; rosters hang off it.
- `assignment_target(item_id, roster_id NULL, user_id NULL, due_at)`. Lets you set section-specific or individual due dates and overrides. Cells with no target render "—" (not assigned).
- `grade(enrollment_id, item_id, score, attempts, graded_by, at)` and `mastery(user_id, skill_id, level, points, at)`. Skill mastery is keyed to the user, not the enrolment, so it carries across courses.
- `grading_scheme(scope[course|roster], scope_id, weights{category:%}, scale)`. Course weighting is the default and a roster may override it. The resolved scheme is stored with the computed total so totals can be audited.
- `saved_view(owner_id, name, view_type, row_filters, col_filters, shared)`.

**Gradebook views**
- **Course × Students** with an audience filter (All / Individuals only, i.e. no roster / a specific roster), plus status, org unit, and date filters.
- **Roster × Assignments** across all courses the roster takes.
- **Student transcript**: every enrolment, grades and mastery, with roster badges.
- **Skill mastery**: students × skills, or a per-unit bar.
- **Org roll-up**: district → school → class. This is the current Schools tab.

**Permissions:** a teacher or leader sees only rosters where they are `teacher`/`leader`. An analyst is read-only. Admins see everything, including individuals who belong to no roster.

**Commerce link:** a seat purchase creates a roster with `seats=N`, and the buyer becomes `leader`, as LifterLMS does. A single purchase creates an enrolment with `source=purchase` and no roster.

### Closest to OhMyLMS (selling courses *and* serving schools/classes)
1. **LearnWorlds** is the closest overall. It combines individual sales, seats, client groups with managers, a Training Matrix, and a gradebook available to reporting roles.
2. **Thinkific (Grow)** and **Teachable (Bulk Distribution)** combine individual sales with B2B seats and group reporting, but their gradebooks are thinner.
3. **Khan Academy Districts** is closest on the school side (per-student seats, class → school → district reporting) but does not sell courses to individuals.
4. **Canvas** and **Brightspace** have the most flexible roster gradebooks but are built for institutions, not commerce.
5. **Kajabi** is commerce-first with cohorts, and has no real gradebook.
6. **TalentLMS, Docebo, Absorb and 360Learning** are corporate-oriented; their hierarchy and group reporting is a good pattern for the district/school roll-up.

**Takeaway for OhMyLMS:** no single SaaS product we found combines course commerce, K-12 rosters and a Canvas-style gradebook. The gap OhMyLMS can fill is the LearnWorlds commerce and seats model plus Canvas row/column filters and presets plus Khan-style mastery and district roll-up.
